# Customer-Churn-Retention-and-RFM-Analysis-Dasboard
Customer churn and declining customer engagement can significantly impact e-commerce revenue and long-term business growth. This project analyzes customer churn and retention patterns while using RFM (Recency, Frequency and Monetary) analysis to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior and value. The dashboard aims to identify customers at risk of churn, understand retention patterns, identify high-value customer segments, and generate actionable insights to support targeted retention strategies.



Project Objectives

Analyze customer churn patterns and identify high-risk customers
Measure customer retention and repeat-purchase behavior
Perform RFM analysis to segment customers based on value and engagement
Identify VIP, loyal, potential and at-risk customers
Analyze revenue contribution across customer segments
Identify factors influencing customer churn
Generate actionable business recommendations for improving retention
