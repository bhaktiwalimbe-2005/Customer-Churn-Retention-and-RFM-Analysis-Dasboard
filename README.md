# Customer Churn, Retention & RFM Analytics

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes customer churn, retention, purchasing behavior, and customer value using **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis**.

The dashboard is designed for an e-commerce business to identify customers at risk of churn, understand retention patterns, segment customers based on purchasing behavior, and generate actionable business recommendations for improving customer retention and revenue.

---

## Problem Statement

Customer churn and declining customer engagement can significantly impact e-commerce revenue and long-term business growth.

This project analyzes:

* Customer churn patterns
* Customer retention and repeat-purchase behavior
* Customer purchasing frequency and monetary value
* RFM-based customer segments
* Revenue contribution across customer segments
* Factors influencing customer churn

The dashboard aims to identify customers at risk of churn, understand retention patterns, identify high-value customer segments, and generate actionable insights to support targeted retention strategies.

---

* Analyze customer churn patterns and identify high-risk customers

* Perform RFM analysis to segment customers based on value and engagement

#  4. RFM Analysis Dashboard

RFM analysis is used to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior.

###  RFM Metrics

**Recency**

Measures how recently a customer made a purchase.

**Frequency**

Measures how frequently a customer makes purchases.

**Monetary**

Measures the amount of money spent by a customer.

###  KPIs

#  5. Insights & Conclusion

The final page summarizes the major findings from the churn, retention, and RFM analysis.

##  Key Insights

### 1. Customer Frequency

Customers with lower purchase frequency show a higher tendency to churn, indicating the importance of repeat-purchase strategies.

### 2. Premium Customers

Premium/VIP customers demonstrate stronger retention and higher monetary contribution compared with other customer groups.

### 3. Regional Performance

Revenue is concentrated in specific geographic markets, highlighting opportunities for targeted regional marketing.

### 4. VIP Contribution

VIP customers contribute significantly to overall revenue and should be prioritized through loyalty and retention programs.

### 5. Customer Tenure

Newer customers show greater churn risk, suggesting that early-stage engagement and onboarding strategies can improve retention.

---

#  Conclusion

The analysis highlights several important customer behavior patterns:

* Churn is higher among new customers.
* Loyal customers generate most of the revenue.
* Retention increases with higher engagement.
* High-frequency customers have the highest lifetime value.

---

#  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies can be implemented:

* **Offer discounts to new customers** to encourage repeat purchases.
* **Introduce loyalty programs** to retain high-value customers.
* **Send personalized recommendations** based on customer purchasing behavior.
* **Target at-risk customers with email campaigns** and personalized offers.
* **Increase engagement in low-performing regions** through targeted marketing campaigns.
